## Logo Usage

### Wordmark

The primary Wazoo wordmark consists of the **W** emoji mark + the word "Wazoo" in Inter.

| Variant | File | Use |
|---------|------|-----|
| Dark background | `logo/dark.svg` | On light backgrounds |
| Light background | `logo/light.svg` | On dark/colored backgrounds |

### Emoji Mark

The standalone W mark (`logo/w.svg`) is a semi-circle composite forming the letter **W** in Wazoo Orange (`#ff9800`). Use it at 16×16px (app icons, tight spaces) or 32×32px+ (hero, print).

### Clear Space

Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the W glyph on all sides.

### Don'ts

- Do not stretch or skew the logo
- Do not change the logo colors outside approved variants
- Do not add effects (drop shadows, glows, gradients) to the logo
- Do not place the logo on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast
