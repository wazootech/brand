## Logo usage

### Wordmark

The primary Wazoo wordmark consists of the **W** glyph mark + the word "Wazoo" in Inter.

| Variant | File | Use |
|---------|------|-----|
| Dark background | `assets/dark/wazoo.svg` | For `#040404` backgrounds |
| Light background | `assets/light/wazoo.svg` | For `#FAFAF9` backgrounds |

The standalone W Mark is available in themed variants in `assets/light/` and `assets/dark/`. Use the variant that matches the background contrast.

### Clear space

Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the W glyph on all sides.

### Don'ts

- Do not stretch or skew the logo
- Do not change the logo colors outside approved variants
- Do not add effects (drop shadows, glows, gradients) to the logo
- Do not place the logo on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast
