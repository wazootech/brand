# Colors

## Primary

| Token | Hex | RGB | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|-----|
| `wazoo-orange` | `#ff9800` | rgb(255, 152, 0) | Primary brand accent; logo W glyphs, CTAs, links on hover |
| `wazoo-orange-dark` | `#e68a00` | rgb(230, 138, 0) | Pressed/active states of primary |

## Brand Background

| Token | Hex | RGB | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|-----|
| `void` | `#040404` | rgb(4, 4, 4) | Brand background (dark surfaces) |
| `surface` | `#0f0f0f` | rgb(15, 15, 15) | Cards, panels |

## Text

| Token | Hex | RGB | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|-----|
| `text` | `#b0b0b1` | rgb(176, 176, 177) | Body copy on dark backgrounds |
| `text-muted` | `#7c7c7c` | rgb(124, 124, 124) | Secondary text, captions |
| `white` | `#ffffff` | rgb(255, 255, 255) | Headings, high-contrast text |

## Accents

| Token | Hex | RGB | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|-----|
| `highlight` | `#ffaa00` | rgb(255, 170, 0) | Highlight badges, quirky highlight effect |
| `selection` | `#846ce4` | rgb(132, 108, 228) | Text selection background |
| `magenta` | `#ff00ff` | rgb(255, 0, 255) | Glitch effect accent |
| `cyan` | `#00ffff` | rgb(0, 255, 255) | Glitch effect accent |

## Logo Gradient (Worlds W Mark)

The W mark inside the Worlds logo uses a layered semi-circle gradient:

| Layer | Colors | Description |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| Outer ring | `#ffe599` → `#f1c232` | Yellow semi-circles |
| W glyphs | `#ff9800` (×3) + `#ff9800` (connecting stroke) | Wazoo Orange |
