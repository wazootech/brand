## Icons

Icon assets are located in `/images/icons/`. All icons are SVG files styled for monochrome use in the Mintlify dark theme.

## Wordmark

The wordmark is **"Wazoo"** in Inter 600 weight with the W emoji mark (🪐 or the SVG W glyph) to its left in lockups.

## Usage Rules

- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects to any logo asset
- Maintain clear space of at least the height of the W glyph on all sides
- Do not place logo assets on busy photographic backgrounds — use a solid `void` or `surface` background
